# Privacy Policy – "Survivor" game
Last updated: 2026

1. We collect no personal data. The game never asks for your name, email or location.
2. Game progress (level, coins, weapons, settings) is stored only on your device and never sent anywhere.
3. The game has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and does not use the internet.
4. The game requests no permissions (camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage).
5. The game contains fictional violence against zombies and collects no information about children.
6. To delete your data, uninstall the game or clear its data in device settings.
7. If features that need data (such as ads) are added later, this policy will be updated first.
